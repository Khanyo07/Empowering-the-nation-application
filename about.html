About us

Skills training for tomorrow.

Our story

Precious Radebe runs Empowering the Nation from three venues in Johannesburg. The business trains domestic workers and gardeners through six-month learnerships and six-week short skills programmes.

The aim is simple: give people skills they can be paid for, so they can earn more in the work they already do or start working for themselves.

Mission and vision

Mission: to offer affordable, practical courses, taught close to where learners live and work, that lead directly to more income or self-employment.

Vision: a Johannesburg where every domestic worker and gardener has recognised, marketable skills.
